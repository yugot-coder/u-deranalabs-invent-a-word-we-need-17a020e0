## 1. Chairdrobe
Chairdrobe (n.) the chair that holds clothes too clean to wash and too worn to put away.
— 88 chars. Blend chair + wardrobe. The "clothes chair" is near-universal; the word names
an object everyone already owns but no dictionary names. Transparent, pronounceable,
instantly usable in a sentence ("just throw it on the chairdrobe").
